Today's founders, as pitched

The assumption map. Every answer lands here by how much the business needs it to be true, and how strong the proof is. Top left is the danger zone: important and unproven.

Ask about the riskiest assumption, not the most interesting one.

A big projection is a claim. Find the assumption that kills the business if it is wrong, then ask for proof. The strongest proof is what people pay for or commit to, not what they say.

Methods: Business Model Canvas and Testing Business Ideas, by Strategyzer.
