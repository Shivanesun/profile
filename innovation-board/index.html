Evidence before scale.

Headline numbers measure attention. Evidence measures behaviour. The strongest evidence is what people pay for or commit to, not what they say or click. Test cheaply, kill early, and put the big money only where the evidence is strong.

Methods: Business Model Canvas, Value Proposition Canvas, Testing Business Ideas and The Invincible Company, by Strategyzer.
